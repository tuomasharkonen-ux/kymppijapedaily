Chapter 4 · Something groundbreaking October 2026

No more skins.
Something bigger.

Right then Claude Opus 5.5 came out, and it was seriously good at animation and 3D. After some brainstorming with Opus, two ideas stuck. Both are real credit sinks for players who already own everything.

🎟️

Vedot

Bet credits on your own run before the first throw. Odds, a daily pot, a progressive jackpot.

A 3D island on a Finnish lake that you build piece by piece, plus neighbours to visit and prank.

The Mökki Oct 6

Every Finn deserves a mökki

Buy a little island, then build it up: woodshed, sauna, dock, rowing boat, a dog. The 3D scene follows real Finnish seasons and time of day, and the sauna chimney smokes on days you've played.

The whole 3D mökki took
single-digit % of one week's Claude usage, with Opus 5.5 ✨
☀️ Summer day
Life at the mökki

Chores, a dog and neighbours to annoy

🐕 Daily chores pay credits

Go fishing, grill makkara, take your sauna turn, pick berries. Every piece you buy unlocks something to do.

😈 Visit & prank neighbours

TP their birches, hoist a Swedish flag, raid the berries. Their dog might catch you.

📬 …and find out in the mailbox

"Matti hopped into your palju." Visits and gifts both earn a few credits.

The economy

🌳 Mökki Rakennuspuu Tech-tree plan · draft 2026-10-15

The flat Mökki build list becomes a Civilization-style tree. There are 24 builds in 5 branches and 4 eras: the 10 you can build today, plus 14 new ones. Bigger builds unlock daily chores, things to do on neighbors' islands, and perks. Click any node for details. On a locked node, the whole route to it lights up, with its total cost.

Design rules

🏃 GrandfatheringAnything you already own stays, even if the new tree says it came too early. The rules only apply when you buy something. Missing earlier builds can still be bought to fill the gap. Nothing is given away for free.
🔨 Finish before you advanceAn earlier build has to be finished, not just bought. No starting the Palju while the sauna is still scaffolding.
📅 Eras gate by days playedI: 0 · II: 10 · III: 30 · IV: 60. The existing day requirements stay the same. Only Rantasauna changes: it moves from 0 to 10 days.
🎲 Dice stay randomNo perk ever changes the dice or the bet odds. The rewards are small daily chore credits and social fun.
💰 Slow economyA mökki built through phase 4 earns about 20–25 credits a day, plus bonuses from visits. The whole tree costs about 17,000 credits. Every reward is one value in the shared rules file, so it's easy to tune.
♨️ Play firstChores open once you've played today (the sauna is warm). The mökki stays a reward for playing, never a replacement.

🐕 The dog — pick one of three

The Koira costs 700 cr and needs the Riukuaita fence. You choose the breed and give it a name of up to 16 characters. All dogs walk around the island, nap on the porch at night, swim off the laituri in summer and greet visitors. Petting the dog (Rapsuta) gives +1 a day. Each breed also has its own trick.

😈 Neighbor interactions

LimitsEach prank can hit the same mökki once a day. Only one of each kind can be up at a time ("Aino already TP'd this mökki — look!").
Never costs the targetA prank stays until the owner taps to clean it, or for 24 hours. Cleaning up pays the owner +1. Everyone who visits can see it.
Dog defenseThe target's dog may foil the prank: 20% of the time, or 50% for the Aussie. The server rolls the dice.
Mailbox inboxThe porch mailbox finally gets used. It lists what happened to your mökki since your last visit.
"Rauhallinen mökki"An opt-out switch in Customization. It blocks pranks on your mökki, and you can't prank anyone else either. Test users are excluded from pranks.
